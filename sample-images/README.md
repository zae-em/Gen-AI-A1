# 10 dataset samples

These genuine test-dataset examples were extracted at their original 128 x 128 resolution from the imported assignment's lossless evaluation figures.

- pets/: Six Oxford-IIIT Pet images for Universal, Hard-Routed, and Soft MoE restoration. Choose salt-and-pepper, blur, or occlusion in the app to apply damage.
- faces/: Four FS2K photographs for Face-to-Sketch. Choose any of the three styles.
- preview.png: A labeled overview of all ten images.
- sources.json: Dataset IDs, test split, figure locations, and source attribution.

Upload an individual PNG from pets/ or faces/ at http://localhost:3000.
The report figures embed dataset inputs directly; restored/generated outputs were excluded from these sample files.
